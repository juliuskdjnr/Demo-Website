Quick Answers

Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer here? Send us a message above.

How long does delivery take?

We deliver nationwide across Ghana within 48 hours. Accra orders are typically delivered same-day or next morning. You'll receive a tracking link as soon as your order ships.

What is your return policy?

You can return any product within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. The item must be in its original packaging. We'll arrange a free pickup from your location.

Are your products covered by warranty?

Yes — all products come with a minimum 12-month warranty. Some premium items carry a 2-year warranty. Warranty details are listed on each product page.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Mobile Money (MTN, Vodafone, AirtelTigo), bank transfers, and all major credit/debit cards. All payments are secured with 256-bit SSL encryption.

Do you ship outside Ghana?

Currently we deliver within Ghana only. We are actively working on expanding to Nigeria, Côte d'Ivoire, and Senegal. Sign up for our newsletter to be notified first.

How do I track my order?

Once your order is dispatched you'll receive an SMS and email with a tracking link. You can also contact our support team at any time for a live update on your delivery.
